# Hands In The Future Nonprofit Website

## Mission Statement
Hands In The Future is a nonprofit organization dedicated to give 
civilians at risk of being homeless as well as recent graduates an opportunity to find a high-paying
career through educational resources, job site programs, and paid internships.

## Website Purpose
The goal of this website is to provide information about the nonprofit and its programs
as well as accept donations, recruit volunteers, and create contacts with workforces.
It will serve as a digital presence to raise awareness as well as provide employment
for workforces in need of employees.

## Target Audience
- Recently laid-off civilians
- Currently unemployed civilians
- Potential donors and workforce sponsors
- Recently graduated alumni

## Planned Features
- Home page that briefly discusses mission overview alongside highlights
- About us page for history about nonprofit, history, and success stories
- Programs page listing initiatives for target audience
- Events calendar or list
- Donation page with form or link
- Contact form for inquiries for general questions or for workforces inquiry
- Mobile-friendly responsive design
- Embedded map for event locations
